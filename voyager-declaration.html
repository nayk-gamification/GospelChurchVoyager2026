VOYAGER RETREAT
DECLARATION

Our Journey Towards Spiritual Maturity

The leader reads each numbered passage. Everyone responds together.

SECTION 01

Our Journey Towards Spiritual Maturity

01LEADER

We came to this retreat to learn about spiritual maturity. We have learned that God wants us to grow and become more like Jesus. We are still growing, and we have much to learn.

ALL

I am not alone.

02LEADER

We learned that God has placed us in this world to live, study, work, enjoy life and build relationships. These are good things. Yet without God, and without the life that Jesus gives, even our achievements can become meaningless. We want to understand ourselves and this world through the eyes of Jesus and his eternal life.

ALL

I am not alone.

03LEADER

We learned about Jacob, who struggled with God and with himself. His faith was not always mature. Yet God continued to work in his life and change him. We too are still growing.

ALL

I am not alone.

04LEADER

We learned that life will bring trials, disappointments and pain. Sometimes we will be afraid, and sometimes we will not understand what God is doing. Yet even through difficult experiences, God can help us grow.

ALL

I am not alone.

05LEADER

We learned that we all face temptations. We struggle with desires for money, power, fame and relationships that serve only ourselves. We want to recognise these temptations and intentionally fight against them. We ask God to help us overcome the desires that lead us away from him.

ALL

I am not alone.

06LEADER

We learned from Esther that God may seem hidden. There will be times when we do not know what will happen next. We want to learn to wait patiently, trust God and make wise decisions, even when we cannot see what he is doing.

ALL

I am not alone.

SECTION 02

Learning from One Another

07LEADER

Around the campfire, we heard stories from people in our own church. They shared their struggles, their mistakes and how Jesus has worked in their lives. We discovered that others have faced struggles and temptations like ours.

ALL

I am not alone.

08LEADER

We have learned that our mentors, senior youth, parents and friends are also growing. They too have faced fear, anger, failure and disappointment. God has helped them, and through them, God can help us.

ALL

I am not alone.

SECTION 03

Growing in Head, Heart and Hands

09LEADER

Today we learned that spiritual maturity involves our HEAD. We want to grow in knowledge and wisdom. We want to understand God, ourselves and the world better, so that we can recognise what is true and make wise judgments.

ALL

I am not alone.

10LEADER

We learned that spiritual maturity involves our HEART. We all experience anger, fear, insecurity, jealousy and disappointment. We want to become more patient and less angry. We want to learn to handle our emotions without allowing them to control our actions.

ALL

I am not alone.

11LEADER

We learned that spiritual maturity involves our HANDS. Our faith must shape our decisions, our habits, our relationships and our responsibilities. We want to make good choices, do our work well and become people who do good in this world.

ALL

I am not alone.

SECTION 04

Our Commitment

12LEADER

Today we have written down areas where we want to grow. Some of us know what steps to take. Others are still unsure. But we have made a beginning, and we want to keep growing towards spiritual maturity.

ALL

I am not alone.

13LEADER

We want to grow together with the friends we have made here. We want to strengthen our friendships, encourage one another and reach out to our mentors and senior youth. We want to help one another become more like Jesus.

ALL

I am not alone.

14LEADER

We know that we will sometimes fail. There will be days when we struggle with old habits, lose our patience or make wrong decisions. But we can admit our mistakes, seek forgiveness, receive help and continue growing.

ALL

I am not alone.

15LEADER

We remember Paul's words that we are growing together towards maturity in Christ. We are part of his body, the church. God has given us one another, and we will continue this journey together.

ALL

I am not alone.

SECTION 05

Our Hope

16LEADER

Lord Jesus, we have learned much during this retreat. We have also discovered how much we still need to grow. We ask you to continue shaping our thinking, our hearts and our actions.

ALL

I am not alone.

17LEADER

We have learned that whether life is easy or difficult, whether our prayers are answered as we hope or whether we must wait, we can continue trusting you.

ALL

I am not alone.

18LEADER

And above all, Lord, I have learned that whether I see you clearly or cannot see what you are doing, you are still there. You are with me, working in my life, helping me grow and leading me onwards.

ALL

I am not alone.

19LEADER

For you have promised, "Never will I leave you; never will I forsake you." (Hebrews 13:5)

ALL

I AM NOT ALONE. AMEN
