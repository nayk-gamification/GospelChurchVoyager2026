🌿 JOHN 15:5 (NIV) • 4×4 PUZZLE

THE TRUE VINE

4×4 Sliding Scripture & Vine Puzzle

“I am the vine; you are the branches. If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit; apart from me you can do nothing.”
— John 15:5 (NIV)
🧩 How It Works:
  • 4×4 Grid (15 Tiles + 1 Empty Space): The verse is split into 15 numbered vine-and-branch tiles scrambled in a guaranteed-solvable 4×4 grid.
  • Slide Tiles into Order: Tap any tile adjacent to the empty slot (or anywhere in the same row/column) to slide tiles into rows 1 to 4 from left to right.
  • Visual Feedback: Tiles glow emerald green when they land in their correct home spot.
  • Fastest Time Wins: Finish the entire verse as fast as you can to claim the top spot on the retreat leaderboard!

Just For Fun: Your time will not be saved, recorded or added to any team.
